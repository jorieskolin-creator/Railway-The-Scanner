# Scanner Cognitive And Evidence Contract

This note freezes the current epistemic flow so future changes do not blur
metadata lifecycles.

## Cognitive Flow

1. Intent describes company-authored ambition.
2. Five Juries establish current company reality from current company evidence.
3. Diagnosis explains current reality.
4. Foresight describes external pressure from the company-neutral Foresight KB.
5. Strategic Synthesis connects current reality with external pressure.
6. Verification audits each claim according to its contract.

General Summary remains a strict factual snapshot. Diagnosis, Strategic
Synthesis, WHY/WHAT, Pitch, and Detailed Report may use scoped context and
supported propositions as bounded premises. Foresight context can support macro
pressure, but it cannot prove company action.

Historical Scanner scores, old diagnoses, previous scan classifications, and
previous intent-reality gaps are operational history. They are not company
evidence and must not be used as company-facing longitudinal conclusions.

## Evidence Lineage

The expected evidence chain is:

- `SRC` / `EVD`: canonical source record.
- `PRS`: deterministic parsed source object.
- `PASS`: selected support passage.
- `PROP`: atomic source-supported proposition and limitations.
- `PKG`: Jury evidence package.
- `FND`: Jury finding.
- `CLM` / `SYN`: generated diagnosis or synthesis claim.

A source can support one proposition exactly, contextualize another, and fail a
third claim. A failed claim-source relation must not demote the whole source
when the source is live, readable, entity-scoped, and contextually relevant.

## Company Evidence DB Boundary

Company Evidence DB remains admin-managed candidate material. It can provide
useful metadata, cached extracts, and prioritization, but live Scanner still
checks URL, readability, entity scope, contextual fit, allowed Jury use, and
claim/proposition support before use.

No cleanup, deletion, consolidation, or legacy-code removal belongs to this
hardening phase.
