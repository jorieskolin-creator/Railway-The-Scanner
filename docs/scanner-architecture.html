The Scanner Architecture Brief

The Scanner is an agentic market forensics engine, not a chatbot with a dashboard.

It gathers company evidence, verifies entity scope, tests source support, routes synthesis across model chains, scores current and future vulnerability, and records uncertainty when the evidence is not good enough. The important idea is simple: the system decides what the evidence allows before a model is allowed to write.

01Operating Idea

Evidence chooses what the Scanner may say. The models only write inside that boundary.

The Scanner is built around a deliberate constraint: generated prose is downstream of verified evidence, deterministic scores, entity identity, source credibility, and quality state. This is why it can produce strongly suggestive diagnosis and proposals without treating every attractive source as a fact.

The company is an entity, not just a name.

Aliases, former names, legal entities, subsidiaries, owner context, and URL hosts are part of the evidence decision. Same-name companies are blocked instead of blended.

Snippets are not evidence.

A scanner snippet may describe what a model thought it saw. The promoted source quote must come from readable page content and support the claim.

Autonomy is bounded by audit state.

The system can retry, downgrade, skip, fact-check, or save partial results, but it records why those decisions happened.

Market intelligence is a second-order product.

Opportunity maps, FAVI, and cohort summaries are computed from stored company scans rather than invented as a separate narrative layer.

03Agentic Control Loop

The system performs its own analytical control loop before, during, and after writing.

The agentic nature is not about taking external action. It is about internal judgment: detecting weak evidence, choosing safer fallback routes, stopping unsafe promotion, fact-checking its own outputs, and preserving quality labels when the scan cannot fully complete.

Observe

Collect signals

Company URL, uploaded context, historical findings, financials, foresight KB, and web evidence are gathered as candidate material.

Judge

Test evidence

URLs, content, entity match, claim support, source credibility, contradictions, and scope are checked before promotion.

Route

Pick the path

Model chains, fallback providers, shorter synthesis paths, or skip decisions are chosen based on budget and output quality.

Synthesize

Write inside bounds

General Summary, AI Diagnosis, proposals, and reports use only promoted sources and structured scan findings.

Record

Persist audit state

The saved report carries confidence, evidence status, hallucination flags, skip reasons, and maintenance metadata.

Why this is heavy thinking

A single prompt can produce a fluent answer. The Scanner performs a chain of decisions around that answer: whether the evidence is entity-safe, whether the source quote really supports the claim, whether the model should be trusted or fact-checked by a different family, whether time budget permits a deeper report, and whether old stored evidence should remain visible but blocked from generation.

05Evidence Boundary

A live URL is only the first gate. It is not permission to write a company fact.

The most important reliability rule is the evidence ladder. Every source can be transparent in the audit trail, but only a source that reaches the top of the ladder can shape generated analysis.

Gate Question Passing Condition Failure Result
URL verified Does the link resolve? The page is reachable and not merely an error, redirect trap, or dead endpoint. Excluded or unavailable.
Content readable Can source text be extracted? Readable page content exists beyond a crawler guess or old scanner snippet. Unchecked or display-only.
Target entity verified Does the page discuss the scanned company? Content identifies the target company, official host, known alias, legal entity, subsidiary, former name, or explicit owner context. Wrong entity, weak entity, or scoped-only.
Claim supported Does the source support the attached claim? The source quote or extracted text supports the exact claim or a clearly scoped version of it. Unsupported or partial.
Generation eligible Can it influence prose? All earlier gates pass and the promotion status is safe. Visible for audit only.

Allowed

Use verified sources for General Summary, AI Diagnosis, WHY/WHAT, Strategic Pitch, Detailed Report, FAVI context, and exports when entity and claim scope are clear.

Forbidden

Treat a search snippet, generic trend page, same-name company, owner-only page, local legal entity, or unchecked historical finding as unqualified company-wide proof.

Rules of Evidence

The Scanner's evidence protocol is intentionally stricter than ordinary source collection. It separates reachability, readability, entity fit, claim support, source quote, and generation permission. 

SOURCE OF TRUTH:
  Candidate evidence is not trusted until URL, content, entity, and claim gates pass.

URL VERIFIED != ANALYSIS VERIFIED:
  Reachability proves a page exists. It does not prove relevance or support.

CONTENT RULE:
  source_quote / extracted source text outranks scanner snippets and search summaries.

ENTITY RULE:
  Content must identify the target company, official domain, known alias, former name,
  legal entity, subsidiary, or explicit owner context.

SCOPE RULE:
  local_legal_entity, subsidiary, former_name, and parent_owner evidence stays scoped.

FORESIGHT RULE:
  External trend and KB sources describe pressure. They do not prove current company facts.

PROMOTION RULE:
  analysis_verified + promotion_status=generation_eligible + generation_eligible=true
  are required before a source can influence generated prose.

FAILURE RULE:
  wrong_entity, unsupported, unavailable, excluded, unknown entity, or weak entity
  becomes display-only / excluded audit material, not narrative fuel.

Layer Firewall

Each layer has a job, and each layer has something it is not allowed to smuggle into the next stage. This is where the architecture becomes safer than a fluent single-prompt answer.

Entity identity can define target scope, but cannot infer operations, strategy, or performance.
Reality scan can mark findings, but cannot use strategy output as proof of execution.
Evidence gate can promote sources only when URL, content, entity, quote, and claim support align.
General Summary cannot repeat stakeholder inventory, pitch services, or use the Knowledge Base as factual evidence.
AI Diagnosis can interpret verified patterns, but cannot invent internal problems or present weak evidence as confirmed.
WHY / WHAT / Pitch can activate verified gaps, but cannot detach recommendations from real findings.
Foresight can frame external pressure, but cannot assert company behavior from trend context alone.
Admin sanitizers can clean wording and metadata, but cannot imply source verification or silently rewrite reports.

07Foresight And FAVI

Future pressure is structured evidence, not a floating future-story.

Foresight is stored as key forces, cross-impact chains, scenarios, climate exposure, source validation, and FAVI inputs. The company narrative uses those structures only when confidence and credibility are high enough.

Foresight KB

External trend memory

Harvests and verifies trend signals across strategic themes. Trend context can describe market pressure, not company facts, unless entity evidence also exists.

Strategic Foresight

Company pressure model

Maps dimension forces, key drivers, scenarios, climate exposure, and cross-impact logic against the scanned company.

FAVI

Foresight-adjusted vulnerability

Blends future pressure, current vulnerability, adaptation capacity, weak evidence, and reality signals into portfolio-relative segmentation.

Trapped

Low cushion, future bad

Weak adaptation capacity plus high future pressure. Current fragility can compound quickly.

Wealthy Under Pressure

Capacity exists, pressure rising

Financial room exists, but the strategic window is narrowing.

Mature And Stable

Financially fine, lower pressure

Stable position with lower external urgency, though complacency is still checked.

Future-Ready

Best position

Strong adaptation capacity and manageable foresight pressure.

08Market Intelligence

The portfolio layer turns individual company forensics into a market observatory.

Market Intelligence does not start from a blank prompt. It aggregates stored company scans, score distributions, FAVI segmentation, domain patterns, and curated cohorts into views that can be exported and compared.

Sheet Purpose Primary Inputs
Opportunity Map Ranks the most interesting company opportunities across the stored portfolio. Reality gaps, domain health, financial capacity, evidence quality, and company ranking logic.
Executive Summary Summarizes domain health, horizon pressure, top opportunities, and adaptation-capacity patterns. Market health, Three Horizons, FAC x FAVI, and Top 50 opportunity list.
Foresight Vulnerability Shows FAVI company ranking and portfolio-relative vulnerability segments. FAVI, current vulnerability, adaptation capacity, and backend cutoffs.
Sweden Summary Filters the same scoring logic to a curated Sweden company cohort. Normalized company name plus URL host match, filtered opportunities, and filtered FAVI rows.
09Admin Maintenance

Maintenance functions are the long-term memory control layer.

Stored evidence ages. Source pages change. Old snippets can be too confident. Admin maintenance lets the Scanner re-apply newer rules to older intelligence without silently rewriting every narrative.

Verify Evidence

Re-check company sources

Updates URL, content, target entity, claim support, source quote, promotion status, and generation eligibility.

Check Claims

Clean narrative risk

Runs reality checks, flags unsupported claims, and separates evidence problems from format notes.

Harvest KB

Find new trend signals

Searches foresight themes, validates links and content support, and updates the shared KB.

Verify KB

Clean trend memory

Re-validates existing KB signals, removes dead links, and updates analysis-verification metadata.

Sanitize FS

Clean foresight wording

Cleans stored company-level foresight wording and metadata, but does not claim source verification.

Recalc Scores

Refresh deterministic scores

Recalculates financial, reality, FAVI, and derived scores without regenerating text.

Why admin is part of autonomy

These functions are not housekeeping buttons. They are the Scanner's way of improving stored intelligence over time: evidence can be demoted, KB signals can be reverified, false claims can be cleaned, and future regenerations can become safer without pretending old reports were perfect.

1070% vs 99% Difference

The difference is not better prose. It is the machinery around the prose.

A single-prompt analysis can be fluent and still blend weak evidence, old snippets, wrong companies, and generic trend logic. The Scanner is designed to make those failure modes visible and controllable.

70% artifact: polished answer

One model writes from whatever context it has. The answer may sound credible, but there is no durable ladder for entity match, source support, audit state, or future re-cleaning.
