Overview

What Market Intelligence Does

The Market Intelligence module aggregates data from all scanned companies into a single strategic dashboard. Instead of looking at one company at a time, it reveals market-wide patterns: which future forces are creating the most pressure, where the biggest readiness gaps are, and which companies are most vulnerable — both today and looking forward.

It requires no additional scanning or API calls. All intelligence is computed from existing company scan data, including foresight links, KB dimension scores, financial sub-scores, and improvement proposals.

Foresight Engine

From Company Megatrends to Market Intelligence

During each company scan, the Strategic Foresight engine identifies which of 30 predefined themes (organized into 7 categories) are exerting pressure on that specific company. These themes cover the full spectrum from Artificial Intelligence and Climate Change to Geopolitics and Population & Demographics.

The 7 Theme Categories

  • Technology & Digital — AI, Data & Digitalisation, Communication & Media
  • Economy & Business — Business, Finance, Global Economy, Digital Economies, Services
  • Industry & Infrastructure — Manufacturing, Construction, Energy, Logistics, Transport
  • Environment & Resources — Climate, Sustainability, Nature, Food, Water & Oceans
  • Society & Governance — Public Governance, Geopolitics, Security, Values & Ethics, Society
  • People & Well-being — Health, Education, Work & Income, Management & HR, Leisure
  • Science & Demographics — Sciences & Research, Population & Demographics

Each foresight link produced at the company level carries rich metadata:

AttributeDescription
FP ThemeWhich of the 30 strategic themes this force belongs to
KB DimensionWhich company dimension it impacts (Operating Model, Service Portfolio, Values, Strategy, AI Readiness)
Force TypeProbable/Emerging, Plausible/Maturing, Weakening/Declining, or Preposterous/Black Swan
Timeframe6–12 months, 12–24 months, or 24–36 months
CredibilityHigh, Medium, or Low — based on source quality
Source & VerificationURL with validation status and supporting corroborating sources

When Market Intelligence aggregates these links across all scanned companies, it reveals which themes appear most frequently (future demand), how they distribute across dimensions (cross-impact), and how industries differ in their exposure (radar chart).

Core Calculation

FAVI — Foresight-Adjusted Vulnerability Index

FAVI is a proprietary, forward-looking vulnerability score that measures the gap between where foresight signals say a company should be heading versus where it actually is — weighted by the company's financial capacity to adapt.

L1
Foresight Pressure (FPS)

How much change is coming? Calculated from force weight × timeframe urgency × source credibility, averaged across all foresight links.

L2
Readiness Gap (RG)

How prepared is the company? For each KB dimension: gap = foresight pressure − current reality score. Only positive gaps count (pressure exceeds readiness).

L3
Financial Capacity (FAC)

Can they afford to adapt? Composite of Investment Signal (40%), Financial Stability (30%), and Profitability (30%).

FAVI = FPS × (1 − FAC) × RG

Multiplicative model (0–100). All three layers must be high for maximum vulnerability.
An additive fallback is used when foresight data is sparse: 0.35·FPS + 0.35·RG + 0.30·(1−FAC)

Additionally, Megatrend Exposure is computed per company: the fraction of its foresight themes that are also top market-wide megatrends — showing how aligned a company's risks are with the broader market forces.

Strategic Segmentation

2×2 FAVI Segmentation Matrix

Every company is placed in a strategic segment based on two axes: Current Vulnerability (from KB dimension scores) and FAVI Score (forward-looking pressure). Threshold: 40 on each axis.

Critical

High Current + High FAVI
Struggling now and blind to what's coming. Urgent sales opportunities with highest value.

Sleepwalking

Low Current + High FAVI
Looks fine today but forces are building. Ideal for foresight-led advisory conversations.

Wounded but Sheltered

High Current + Low FAVI
Problems are real but the future isn't adding pressure. Standard remediation play.

Safe Harbor

Low Current + Low FAVI
Healthy and future-ready. Potential strategic partner or benchmark.

For Sales

How Sales Benefits

Prioritize Outreach

The Sales Priorities tab ranks companies by demonstrated vulnerability. The FAVI tab adds a forward-looking dimension — helping identify companies that don't look distressed yet but will be soon (Sleepwalking segment).

Lead with Foresight

Future Demand data shows exactly which megatrends are creating pressure in a prospect's industry. This lets sales craft conversations around strategic forces rather than generic pain points.

Quantify the Gap

Every company's FAVI breakdown shows the gap between foresight pressure and current readiness per KB dimension. This translates directly into a compelling, data-backed sales narrative.
