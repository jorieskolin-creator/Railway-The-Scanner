The Scanner Foresight Methodology

Foresight creation is a verified signal system, not a future-story generator.

The Scanner creates foresight by separating three intellectual acts: harvesting external signals into a controlled Foresight Knowledge Base, synthesizing company-specific implications from analysis-verified signals, and converting those signals into structured forces, scenarios, source links, diagnosis context, and FAVI pressure. The governing rule is simple: trend evidence can describe external pressure, but it cannot prove current company behavior.

30 Foresight themes

Macro domains of change, from AI and data to climate, finance, governance, work, and demographics.

5 KB dimensions

Operating Model, Strategy, Values, Service Portfolio, and AI Readiness absorb external pressure.

4 Evidence gates

URL liveness, source credibility, content support, and generation eligibility govern promotion.

2 Use layers

Company foresight evidence and portfolio-level FAVI / Market Intelligence are produced from the same stored signals.

01Thesis

The Scanner treats foresight as external pressure mapped against company readiness.

Foresight is not a forecast that claims to know the future. It is an evidence discipline: a signal is harvested from authoritative sources, checked, classified, stored, and then used as context for how a company may need to interpret its present reality.

Foresight is company-neutral at source.

KB signals are marked as foresight_context, company_specific=False, and not_evidence_of_deployment=True. They are trend pressure, not proof of company action.

Company relevance is synthesized later.

The company scan maps verified KB signals to domain themes, priority dimensions, current reality, archetype, financial capacity, and top findings.

Evidence gates precede narrative.

Only analysis-verified, generation-eligible KB signals enter foresight synthesis. Weak or unverified sources remain audit material.

Structured evidence outranks prose.

The primary artifact is not a future essay; it is dimension_forces, cross_impact_chain, scenarios, climate_exposure, and foresight_links.

02End-To-End Pipeline

The foresight system has two loops: a knowledge-base loop and a company-scan loop.

The KB loop gathers and cleans external trend signals. The company loop reuses only promoted KB signals to create company-specific foresight, avoiding uncontrolled live search during normal scans.

Foresight creation path

Company-level foresight is created from the Scanner's verified Foresight Knowledge Base. External signals are first harvested, validated, content-checked, deduplicated, classified, and stored at KB level. Company foresight then synthesizes only those governed signals against the scanned company's verified reality, financial capacity, intent-reality gaps, and strategic context. This keeps foresight creation evidence-led rather than dependent on ad hoc live-search results during each company scan.

03Foresight KB Harvest

The KB harvest is the controlled research gate for new external information.

The harvester scans the 30-theme taxonomy, but it does not simply save whatever the model finds. It applies source targeting, SIFT-style validation, URL checks, credibility scoring, content verification, deduplication, and merge rules.

04Company-Level Synthesis

Why the company path is KB-only

This is a reliability choice. Company scans already have many moving parts: entity verification, reality evidence, summary generation, fact-checking, and exports. By moving live search to the KB harvest, the Scanner reduces hallucinated URLs and keeps company-level foresight inside a previously verified evidence universe.

05Key Force Anatomy

A key force is not just a trend label. It is a structured argument.

Each force binds a macro signal to a Scanner knowledge dimension, a company-specific implication, a time horizon, a source set, lifecycle state, and action timing. That structure is what makes the force usable in reports, diagnosis, FAVI, and Market Intelligence.

Conceptual Object

06Verification And Guardrails

The foresight evidence ladder is deliberately narrower than ordinary web research.

The Scanner is designed to preserve useful weak signals while preventing weak or wrongly scoped sources from becoming generated claims. Verification decides what may influence prose and scoring.

Gate Question Stored Fields Failure Mode
URL verification Does the primary or supporting URL resolve? url_verified, verified_supporting_sources Dead sources are removed, demoted, or blocked.
Credibility assessment Is the domain/source type high, medium, low, or unknown? source_credibility, source_type Confidence is downgraded when source quality is weak.
Content verification Does readable source content support the signal claim? content_verified, claim_support_status, source_quote Unchecked or unsupported content becomes display-only/excluded.
Promotion gate Can the signal enter generated analysis? analysis_verified, generation_eligible, promotion_status Non-promoted signals remain visible for audit, not synthesis.
Language guardrail Does foresight overstate certainty or obligations? foresight_sanitized, clean_by_construction, warning lists Deterministic wording is softened to modal, conditional language.

Allowed

"External financial-sector signals suggest pressure from AI governance, which may require banks to evaluate accountability and operating-model readiness if in scope."

Forbidden

"The company is deploying AI governance and must redesign its operating model." A trend source cannot prove current deployment or create a direct obligation by itself.

07Methodological Foundations

The system combines futures studies, OSINT validation, evidence promotion, company-readiness analysis, and a Popper-compatible methods mix.

The implementation is pragmatic rather than purely theoretical, but its logic is academically defensible: classify the external environment, validate sources laterally, map signals to organizational capacities, and distinguish plausible pressure from proven company facts. Rafael Popper's Foresight Diamond is visible here as a background logic: the Scanner combines evidence, expertise, creativity, and limited interaction rather than relying on a single foresight method.

Popper-compatible reading: the Foresight Diamond

The Scanner should not be described as a full participatory foresight programme in Popper's sense. It is not running citizen panels, Delphi rounds, or workshop-based stakeholder deliberation. But the methodological dynamics are clearly aligned with Popper's Foresight Diamond: evidence grounds the signal, expertise frames relevance, creativity opens alternative futures, and interaction appears through review, maintenance, and renewal loops.

Relationship to common strategy frameworks

The Scanner does not reject familiar methods such as PESTEL, SWOT, Porter's Five Forces, scenario planning, or horizon scanning. It treats them as partial lenses. Its own method combines horizon scanning, evidence qualification, entity-aware verification, knowledge-base classification, cross-impact reasoning, and company-specific readiness analysis.

Method Useful For Limitation In This Context How The Scanner Extends It
PESTEL Structuring macro-environmental forces across political, economic, social, technological, environmental, and legal categories. It names categories of change, but does not verify source quality, entity relevance, signal maturity, or whether a pressure connects to a specific company's operating reality. Uses the broader 30-theme foresight taxonomy, then gates each signal through credibility, content support, classification, and company-readiness mapping.
SWOT Communicating strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats in a simple executive form. It can become subjective and static when assumptions, opinions, and verified facts are mixed without source governance. Separates verified company reality from external opportunity and threat signals, then keeps weak, unsupported, or wrong-entity evidence out of generated conclusions.
Porter's Five Forces Assessing industry structure, bargaining power, substitution, rivalry, and entry barriers. It is less sensitive to cross-domain forces such as AI governance, data infrastructure, climate regulation, autonomous operations, work transformation, and ecosystem shifts. Combines industry/domain context with cross-impact chains and priority dimensions, so external pressure can be mapped to operating model, strategy, service portfolio, values, and AI readiness.
Scenario planning Exploring plausible alternative futures and strategic uncertainty. It can become speculative if scenarios are not tied back to evidence quality, timeframe, source credibility, and present company constraints. Creates micro-scenarios only from qualified forces, then links them to evidence status, action timing, risk/reward framing, and readiness limits.
Horizon scanning Detecting weak signals, emerging issues, and trend momentum before they become mainstream planning assumptions. Raw scanning can over-collect links and promote loosely related material if there is no strict evidence-promotion boundary. 08FAVI And Market Use

Foresight becomes quantitative pressure only after it has been converted into validated foresight links.

The FAVI layer does not score raw prose. It uses foresight_links extracted from dimension forces, where each link carries theme, dimension, timeframe, force type, credibility, validation status, and priority-dimension metadata.

Foresight Pressure Score: signal_strength = geometric_mean(force_weight, timeframe_urgency, credibility_weight) FPS = average(signal_strength across validated priority-dimension links) Readiness Gap: gap_i = pressure_i * (1 - reality_score_i / 100) readiness_gap can be adjusted upward by current vulnerability signals FAVI: if sufficient data: FAVI = geometric_mean(FPS, 1 - FAC, readiness_gap, megatrend_exposure_factor) * 100 if sparse data: FAVI = additive fallback using pressure, readiness gap, financial capacity, megatrend exposure, and challenge density

Market Intelligence reuse

At portfolio level, Market Intelligence counts top foresight themes, builds theme-by-dimension cross-impact matrices, computes foresight coverage, ranks opportunity maps, and segments companies by current vulnerability and FAVI. The same company-level foresight_links therefore support both individual reports and the market observatory.

09Boundaries And Reliability

The most important reliability feature is what the foresight system refuses to claim.

The Scanner can produce forceful strategic implications, but its reliability model explicitly separates external trend context from company-specific evidence. This is why foresight can guide diagnosis without becoming a hallucinated company fact.

What foresight can do

Identify validated external pressures, map them to company readiness dimensions, express timing and momentum, build scenarios, inform AI Diagnosis, and feed FAVI.

external pressurescenario contextreadiness gap

What foresight cannot do

Prove that a company has deployed a capability, is non-compliant, must act by a date, or has a current internal problem unless separate company evidence supports it.

no deployment proofno hard obligationno invented fact
Generated

At least three live generation-eligible KB signals exist and synthesis returned structured forces and scenarios.

Skipped

Low Trust

A source may remain visible for audit but be blocked from generated prose.

Scoped

A force may be valid as external context but cannot become a company fact without corroboration.

Auditable

The saved report retains sources, validation summary, flags, force metadata, and foresight links.

Bottom line

The Scanner's foresight method is best understood as a layered argument: a verified external signal becomes a classified force; the force is mapped to a company readiness dimension; the dimension pressure is interpreted through current reality and financial capacity; and only then does it influence diagnosis, scenarios, FAVI, and Market Intelligence. In Popper's terms, this is a methods mix with a strong evidence and expertise bias, meaningful creativity in scenarios and weak-signal logic, and a lighter interaction layer through review and renewal. The academic strength of the method is not that it predicts the future. It is that it preserves the distinction between evidence of change in the world and evidence of change inside a company.
